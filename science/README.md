# Scientific account

The unknown qubit direction is a *resource*, not a supplied classical parameter. A universal pure-state concentrator can convert six ideal copies into one fixed CCZ state, but independent input noise fills sectors outside the identical-pure-state subspace. Consequently exact pure-state conversion does not automatically imply first- or higher-order noise suppression.

1. [Model](model.md): accepted maps, iid depolarizing input, exactness, conditional infidelity, distance promise, and charged copies.
2. [Results](results.md): optimal joint order/yield law and exact finite bounds.
3. [Converse](converse.md): positive low-fault sectors, full rejection kernel, binomial envelope, six roots, symmetric geometry.
4. [Construction](construction.md): inherited six-copy concentration, free CCZ twirling, inherited deterministic correcting code, buffering and all-order rounding.
5. [Finite protocols](finite_protocols.md): explicitly specified 48-copy quadratic witness and distinctions between joint and retained-output errors.
6. [Interpretation](interpretation.md): why universality ties robustness to yield, and which resource assumptions matter.
